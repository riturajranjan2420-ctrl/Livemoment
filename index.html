A LITTLE UNIVERSE

Press
and feel.

Nothing to read. Just let it unfold.

01 · Somewhere

Maybe some moments
are meant to be felt.

Not understood.
Not explained.
Just felt.

02 · Slowly

And sometimes
everything gets quiet.

The world keeps moving, but for a moment, you don't feel the need to chase it.

03 · A thought

There is beauty
in not having all the answers.

Some things can simply exist without being explained.

04 · Tonight

Let everything
slow down.

Breathe.
Look around.
Stay for a while.

05 · Remember

You don't have to
carry everything.

Some weight can be put down. Some thoughts can wait. Some days simply need to pass.

06 · Somewhere between

Maybe peace
isn't silence.

Maybe it is simply finding a moment where your mind doesn't have to fight anything.

07 · A little reminder

Keep the things
that make you feel alive.

The music.
The memories.
The little conversations.
The quiet moments.

08 · And then

Let the moment
be enough.

No explanation.
No expectation.
Just this moment.

09 · Finally

Somewhere in all
the noise...

there are still little pieces of peace waiting to be noticed.

10 · That's it

Stay soft.
Stay curious.
Keep going.

The universe is still unfolding.
